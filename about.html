MY ITALIAN JOURNEY

Ciao! I'm Divya ❤️

An Italian language learner, just like you!

My journey with Italian started in 2018...

In October 2018, I came to Italy with big dreams, new experiences waiting for me, and a completely new language to learn.

At that time, Italian was unfamiliar to me. I had to start from zero — learning simple words, understanding grammar, practising conversations and slowly becoming more comfortable.

Like many people learning a new language, I made mistakes, forgot words, struggled with grammar and sometimes found it difficult to express myself.

But I kept going!

Over time, I discovered that learning Italian doesn't always have to be complicated. Sometimes, a simple trick, an everyday example or a little practice can make a big difference.

And here I am, still learning, still improving and still discovering something new about Italian and life in Italy. ❤️

A LITTLE ABOUT THIS CLASSROOM

Why Italian in Minutes?

I created Italian in Minutes to share the simple tricks, tips and learning methods that have helped me along the way.

Here, we'll learn Italian through:

  • Simple grammar explanations
  • Everyday vocabulary and useful expressions
  • Short stories and practical examples
  • Fun quizzes and exercises
  • Useful tips for everyday life in Italy

I'm not here to tell you that I know everything. I'm here to share what I learn, what works for me and what might make your journey easier too.
